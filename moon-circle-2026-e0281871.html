Who Is This For

This circle is for you if:

💤

You feel exhausted and need deep rest, need to regulate sleep patterns and make space for your dreams

🌙

It's important for you to feel more into your own rhythms, connecting more with your body

💜

You're looking for a safe space without judgment, where you can be heard and simply be yourself

🤲

Be supported in the practice of receiving, trusting your intuition and connecting to your creative flow

✨

You want to connect with feminine power gently, not through effort

🦥

You long for a practice that honors slowness and presence over productivity

Our Intention

Why We Gather

This circle is about presence, stillness, bodily sensation, and honest contact with what is alive within. We meet in the rhythm of the Moon to support the natural cycles: rest, manifestation, release, and nourishment.
