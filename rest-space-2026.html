About

Staying connected to your inner self

Rest is so essential for us to stay connected to our innermost, real, and vulnerable selves. At the Solstice and the Full Moon, outer energies reach their peak, but what about our inner energies?

💤

Deep rest helps us stay connected to our innermost, real, and vulnerable selves.

🌞

We will notice whether we are slowing down enough to protect our inner flame in high-intensity times.

🤍

This is a nurturing container of deep holding, love, and protection for our authentic selves.

Intention

A nurturing container for your authentic self

I am aware that when I show up fully, when my energy is at its peak, I am also extremely raw and vulnerable. In those moments, I need deep holding, love, and protection. Inspired by this, I wanted to create a circle that serves as a nurturing container to support our innermost, authentic selves, ground us in our inherent worth, and provide the safety to share our beauty with the world.

Our Flow Together

How we will gather

🧘
We will gather to open our sacred circle and settle into the space
🛌
I will guide you through a Yoga Nidra practice in the Daring to Rest style, to bring you into a state of deep, restorative rest
💭
Then we will have time for quiet reflection, and space to gather and share our insights, only if it feels right for you

The format is soft and gentle: you can stay at your own pace and share only if you want to.
